FAQ

Common questions.

What makes Swatchly different from a regular color picker?

Regular color pickers let you grab a color — then it's gone when you close the popup. Swatchly lets you save colors and fonts into named palettes that persist across sessions, and then apply them with one click to any element on any website. The sidebar stays open as you work across tabs, so your palette tray is always at hand.

Does Swatchly require an account?

No. Swatchly works entirely within your browser using local storage. No sign-up, no server, no cloud sync required.

Which browsers are supported?

Swatchly is a Chrome extension and requires Chrome 95 or later. Support for other Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave) is planned.

Will my palettes still be there after I restart Chrome?

Yes — palettes are stored in Chrome's extension storage and persist indefinitely across restarts. They are tied to your browser profile.

What does "Inject brand into AI" (✦) do?

It copies your palette as enriched JSON — HEX, RGB, HSL for each color, plus font roles, families, sizes, and weights — with a pre-written prompt. Paste it directly into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot to instantly ground your AI design brief in your client's real brand palette.

Can I apply colors to any element on any website?

Yes — Swatchly injects a lightweight content script when you activate Apply mode. Click any visible DOM element to apply your selected palette color as text color or background. For elements that can't be directly targeted, HEX is always copied to clipboard as a fallback.
