FAQ

Common questions.

Does Swatchly require an account?

No. Swatchly works entirely within your browser using local storage. No sign-up, no server, no cloud sync required.

Which browsers are supported?

Swatchly is a Chrome extension and requires Chrome 95 or later. Support for other Chromium-based browsers (Edge, Brave) is planned.

Are my palettes saved if I reinstall or switch computers?

Palettes are stored in Chrome's local extension storage and are tied to your browser profile. They persist across browser restarts but are not synced across devices on the Free plan.

What does "Copy for AI" do?

The ✦ button copies your palette as enriched JSON — including HEX, RGB, HSL values for every color, plus font roles, families, sizes, and weights — along with a suggested prompt for paste into ChatGPT, Gemini, or Copilot for color theory or accessibility analysis.

Can I apply colors to any element on any website?

Yes — Swatchly injects a lightweight content script when you activate Apply mode. Click any visible DOM element to apply your selected palette color as text or background. For elements that can't be directly accessed, HEX is always copied to clipboard.
