Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Data We Collect

Swatchly does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. All palettes, colors, and fonts you save are stored locally in your browser's extension storage (chrome.storage.local) and never leave your device.

Permissions Used

  • storage — to save your palettes and preferences locally.
  • activeTab — to scan the current page's colors and fonts when you request it.
  • scripting — to inject the apply-mode content script when you use the Apply tab.
  • sidePanel — to display the persistent sidebar.

Third-Party Services

Swatchly does not use any analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party services that receive your data.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@swatchly.app.
